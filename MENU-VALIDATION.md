Prepared November 17, 2026 on `feat/interactive-menu-planning`. Hosting remains GitHub Pages; there are no new dependencies. This feature needs the additive `supabase/migrations/20261117132928_interactive_menu_planning.sql` migration after the four existing migrations. **It has not been applied to hosted Supabase.** No live menus, guests, passwords, payments, or agreements were changed.

## Behavior and pricing

Section **08 · Our menu** supports editable courses, multiple selected dishes per course, tasting ratings, notes, private optional photos, and individual vendor links. Group/filter by vendor or course, search dishes, and open a vendor's dishes from Vendors & quotes. Courses can offer one guest choice per attending person or shared service such as buffet/drinks. Photos are JPEG/PNG/WebP up to 5 MB, authenticated through a private `menu-photos` bucket.

| Pricing basis | Quantity used |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed | One package price |
| Per planning guest | Wedding planning headcount, including an explicit zero; an unset count is unknown |
| Per confirmed choice | Active attending guests with a valid choice of this dish; alternatives do not each multiply by the full headcount |
| Unit × planned quantity | Explicit whole-number quantity and descriptive unit |

Selected dish costs are additive. Shared per-person dishes each use the planning count. For alternative plated dishes, use confirmed-choice pricing or distribute planned quantities across dishes. Add service, tax, or other catering charges as selected fixed package lines where applicable; the app does not infer them. Prices are integer minor currency units. Blank price is unknown, zero is real, and the complete estimate is withheld when a needed price/headcount/guest choice is unknown, no dishes are selected, or the sum exceeds 1,000,000,000 cents. Known partial costs remain visible. Per-choice estimates cover confirmed diners, not unreturned RSVPs; shared per-person estimates use the planning headcount.

Link **one existing expense** through Choose catering expense. The menu supplies that unbooked expense's Forecast ahead of its quote or budgeted amount; it never creates another bill. Quotes/targets remain saved. Booked agreement price, payments, and contract history are unchanged by menu edits. A revised estimate appears beside the agreement; changing the contract requires the existing explicit amendment flow. Unlinking restores ordinary quote/target behavior. Deleting the linked expense clears the link.

Guests with RSVP Yes in active households can choose one selected dish per choice-offering course or explicitly stay pending. This is partner-entered, with the same wedding membership access as the rest of the planner, not guest-facing RSVP access. Use Choose meals on the guest list or meal manifest, save a course, then reopen for another course. The guest's table label, dietary flags/request, and caterer confirmation remain distinct from the meal preference. Counts include named guests and unnamed companions once per person. Declined/deleted/archived guests and households are excluded. Choice history remains when RSVP changes; if they attend again, a still-valid choice is reused. Deselection/deletion makes old choices need review rather than silently substituting a dish.

The manifest sorts by table label and guest, includes shared dishes, shows counts per selected dish and missing choices, and offers one caterer CSV plus a print layout. A table label is a simple catering handoff field; visual seating remains a separate future feature. Dietary suitability is never inferred from a dish name or preference. CSV text is escaped against spreadsheet formula execution. Keep exported dietary information private.

Deleted courses/dishes use the existing confirmed tombstone/history system. Course deletion removes its dishes; guest-choice records remain in private history. Vendor deletion detaches dishes, preserving them, although the existing vendor deletion behavior still removes that vendor's linked expenses/payments. Superseded/unlinked photo files are not physically purged; they remain private to wedding members while the dish is active. Deleted dish/course photos become inaccessible. Storage garbage collection is not part of this release.
